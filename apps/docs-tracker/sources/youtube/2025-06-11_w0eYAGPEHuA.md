What the world wants to see more of is the perspective of the artist. What I try to do is make something that was not possible before. What I'm not trying to do is have AI write a poem that I could have written on my own or draw a picture that I could have drawn on my own. These technologies are so new that it's really easy to come in with a unique perspective and make something that the world hasn't seen before. I'd encourage more artists to do that. Like engage with AI with an open mind and think about what they might want to do that wasn't possible before without it. Be as weird as you can get with these new tools. I think people respond to that sort of weird unique experimental energy and I think we all need more of that.
