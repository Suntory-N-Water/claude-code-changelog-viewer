You're a philosopher at Anthropic. Why is it that there's a philosopher at Anthropic? I mostly focused on the character of Claude, how Claude behaves, and I guess some of the more kind of like nuanced questions about how AI models like should behave, things like how should they feel about their own position in the world, and so trying to both teach models how to be like good. I sometimes think of it as like how would the ideal person behave in Claude's situation. It does feel like that at least should be kind of the aspirational goal. These models are being put in positions where they're having to make really hard decisions. I think that just as you want models to be like extremely good at like math and science questions, you also want them to show the kind of like ethical nuance that like we would all broadly think is like very good.
