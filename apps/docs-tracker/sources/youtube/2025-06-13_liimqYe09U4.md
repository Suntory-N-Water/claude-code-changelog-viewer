There's that saying, born too late to explore the earth, born too soon to explore outer space, born just in time to explore large language models. Sometimes people ask me like, "Are you afraid of AI? What's it going to do to design?" One of the ways I like to describe AI is AI makes writer's block optional. You know, you discover something really hard and personal through writer's block and sometimes you shouldn't even try to erase that. But sometimes you should not be stuck and often times the AI isn't giving you the idea you used. But once you get some feedback that helps you realize things that you didn't yet have an opinion about.
